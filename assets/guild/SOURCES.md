# Guild image sources

`guild_cafe_topdown_01.png` is the directly overhead Seoul guild café map generated with the built-in image tool (`exec-6c53fbd7-2677-42d8-b825-8dd8a5112697.png`): a bright blue-gray operational café with a broad walkable center, counter, gate terminal, training corner and recovery corner, viewed at 90 degrees. The WebP is its runtime export.

`guild_operator_han_seoa_illustration_01.png` (`exec-1732a816-449c-4e6c-b979-cf6ad6589063.png`) and `guild_operator_han_seoa_topdown_01.png` (`exec-6de0ef99-7af1-42ca-b396-47e9342b7155.png`) were generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-27. Prompts: an original cheerful adult Korean guild operations coordinator with short brown bob, amber hair clip, headset, blue-gray jacket and teal/amber magical pogo stick, transparent full-body illustration; then the same character as a compact overhead guild sprite with her head, torso, hands, feet and pogo facing one direction. The top-down sprite was alpha-cropped; both have optimized WebP runtime copies.

Four-direction pogo set: the original `guild_operator_han_seoa_topdown_01.png` is the down-facing master; `guild_operator_han_seoa_right_01.png` (`exec-7009edd3-bd25-4134-9286-5e78e34d10bf.png`) and `guild_operator_han_seoa_up_01.png` (`exec-517b92f2-56ad-48a2-86d0-894c59bc51be.png`) were generated with the built-in image tool from that reference, preserving the same clothing, hair clip, headset and spring pogo while turning the whole pose to screen-right or away from camera. `guild_operator_han_seoa_left_01.png` is a horizontal mirror of the right-facing master to preserve exact proportions. The prompt for each generated direction requested one coherent overhead chibi rider with a visible handle, platform, helical spring and rubber ground tip on a transparent canvas. `scripts/build-guild-operator-pogo.py` derives four upper rider/footplate WebP layers plus common spring and tip WebP layers. At runtime the direction image switches while the spring, tip, upper rider and shadow move independently through compression, lift and landing.

Earlier overhead walking attempts for Song Jinwoo and Kang Taehoon were discarded. The guild now renders only Han Seoa as a moving character; combat characters remain available through their existing game systems. Older seated quarter-view image files remain as archival sources and are not rendered.

`guild_hall_cafe_04.png` was generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-27 from `guild_hall_cafe_03.png` (`exec-78c50549-198f-4c3a-9cdd-f5475c9f29ac.png`); the WebP is a quality-86 runtime export. Prompt: make the café the main identity of the same quarter-view Seoul guild room, with a full-width pastry and coffee counter, warm pendants, small edge tables, blue-gray steel framing and Seoul windows; leave the center and lower floor open for controllable characters and separate facilities, and ground every object. No people, UI, logos or readable lettering in the background.

`guild_hall_cafe_03.png`, `song_cafe_01.png`, and `kang_cafe_01.png` were generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-27; the same-name WebP files are quality-88 runtime exports. Source outputs: `exec-158bf68e-9b1a-4f0b-8d92-35f7008b83f9.png`, `exec-6998f759-6a77-47ac-a864-a9e7de15db53.png`, `exec-fc4f98be-3e8a-44c4-a29c-f3a358d25da5.png`.

- Hall prompt: expand the blue-gray Seoul guild workshop into a larger quarter-view room matching the existing painterly-pixel navy/cyan facility cutouts; keep the center and lower floor open, add a small café with counter, pastry display and two tables at the upper-right edge; leave separate facilities and characters out of the background.
- Song café prompt: retain Song Jinwoo's teal jacket and dark hair from the combat reference; show him seated off his scooter with coffee and bread, matching the guild's chibi pixel-painterly style, transparent background and grounded chair feet.
- Kang café prompt: retain Kang Taehoon's dark hair and olive tactical clothing from the combat reference; show him seated off his truck with mug and pastry, same style, transparency and grounded chair feet.

The `*_casual_02.png` room and facility masters were generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-27, then encoded as WebP (quality 88) for runtime. The room prompt requested a bright, hand-painted/pixel-art Seoul guild workshop with a broad empty floor, clear walls/windows and no interactive furniture; source `exec-96cea461-ceed-43bf-974d-a80087372fb3.png`. Training (`exec-8e08b540-84bd-414e-8c49-ccc32adc650b.png`), recovery (`exec-67b1d05e-9918-4ea0-b186-183a33537717.png`), supply (`exec-e63b6b63-ff76-469d-bfba-d43fed35e743.png`), gate terminal (`exec-b3604cf5-5583-4190-ae04-7d35a8c980a7.png`) and roster kiosk (`exec-3f7d14ae-ca6d-45ae-bd80-819466460595.png`) prompts requested matching transparent quarter-view cutouts with visible ground-contact feet and no baked floor or text. These cutouts now appear in facility panels only; the top-down café floor has its own painted furnishings.

The original guild images were generated for Seoul Hero with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-25. The prompts requested a grounded blue-gray modern Seoul fantasy guild room and transparent facility cutouts, with no baked text. `guild_hall_01.png` is the earlier dark room; `guild_rail_foreground_01.webp` is its old masked front-rail layer.

`guild_hall_seoul_day_01.png` was generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-26 from `guild_hall_01.png` (`exec-62aaab0d-f49c-4a35-bea0-99c7150f1799.png`). Prompt: preserve the isometric room footprint, open walkable floor and window positions; change the dark workshop into a bright overcast modern Seoul guild workshop with blue-gray concrete, steel and city views; leave the floor empty for separate facility sprites; avoid fantasy-hotel decor, people, text and logos. This is an archived earlier backdrop.

`guild_roster_kiosk_01.png` was generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-26 (`exec-915851b8-eed7-4ffa-951a-1f0682f4be42.png`). Prompt: transparent grounded blue-gray steel guild-membership kiosk, portrait terminal and registry folder fixed to a waist-high counter, three-quarter top-down view, broad feet at bottom edge, no floating book, people, logos or background. It replaces the free-floating lobby ID-card art inside the guild room.

Matching WebP files for the new room, roster kiosk and three facility cutouts are runtime exports from their PNG masters (Pillow quality 88). The image manifest selects WebP first; the editable PNGs remain in this folder.

`guild_cafe_topdown_02.png` was generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-09-27 (`exec-3a6862ee-5fbc-4ea9-9052-e11cecde4b0c.png`). References: `guild_cafe_topdown_01.webp` for the overhead room geometry and `guild_operator_han_seoa_illustration_01.webp` for the crisp illustrated material style. Prompt: preserve the café counter, training corner, recovery corner, left gate terminal and broad central floor; render sharp modern Seoul guild materials, grounded equipment and subtle gold/teal floor borders distinguishing facilities, no characters or baked text. `guild_cafe_topdown_02.webp` is a 3344×1882 Lanczos/UnsharpMask runtime export (WebP quality 90); the PNG is kept as the image-generation master.

`guild_hall_casual_01.png` is the newer generated casual guild room master from the interrupted redesign. It bakes each facility and its appropriately sized floor shadow into one grounded room image. `guild_hall_casual_01.webp` is the optimized runtime copy produced by `scripts/optimize-casual-art.py`. The separate facility cutouts and old background remain as source assets but are not rendered in this version.
