# Combat chibi cutouts

The `*_casual_01.png` masters in this directory were made during the interrupted casual-art redesign as grounded, transparent stylized combat cutouts. Their WebP runtime copies are generated by `scripts/optimize-casual-art.py`; the original PNGs are retained for future art edits. Rendering alignment and floor shadows are configured in `src/rendering/CombatArt.ts` and `src/rendering/EntitySprites.ts`.
